Wortkette

A chain of German words rolls toward the hole. Your brick carries a Romanian or English word. Shoot it next to its German translation: three of a kind and the chain breaks.

TrainingNew words are taught before each level. Learn them, then break the chain with them.
Aim with meaningEvery colour is one German word (see the legend). Your brick only shows its meaning: you have to know which colour it belongs to.
Wrong shotsA brick that lands next to the wrong word turns its true colour and stays in the chain as clutter.
CombosWhen a gap closes with the same colour on both sides, the chain pulls back and can break again.

Aim: mouse or ← → · Shoot: click or ↑ · Swap brick: right click or Space · Pause: Esc
Touch: drag to aim, let go to shoot · tap the shooter to swap
