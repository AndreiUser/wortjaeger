Wortsprung

Run, jump and learn. Golden scrolls teach you new words. Word gates block the way until you bump the block with the right German word.

Golden scrollsTouch one to learn a new word: the Romanian or English meaning and its German.
Word gatesThe prompt hangs over the gate. Jump into the block with the right German word to open it. A wrong block costs a heart.
Shielded nounsSome gates then ask for the article: bump der, die or das.
HazardsStomp the crawlers, mind the gaps. Falling sends you back to the last flag.

Move: ← → or A / D · Jump: Space, ↑ or W (hold to jump higher) · Close a card: Space or click · Pause: Esc
